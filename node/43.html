    Why get a Mentorship?

    Bridge the Gap Between the Classroom and the Field

    Passing your home inspection exams and memorizing textbooks is only half the battle. When you step onto a real property with a client watching, real-world experience is what gives you confidence.
    I provide hands-on, practical field mentorship designed specifically for new and graduating home inspectors. Instead of learning through trial and error on your own, you’ll get direct 1-on-1 guidance, tool practice, and real property exposure with full explanations.

    What You’ll Learn / Field Experience (Key Benefits)
    Practical Field Coaching Tailored to You

    • One-on-One On-Site Walkthroughs: Accompany me on simulated field inspections to learn standard operating procedures, defect recognition, and real-world workflow.
    • Advanced Tool Practice: Get hands-on experience using essential industry equipment—including thermal imaging (IR cameras), moisture meters, and aerial inspection tech—so you know how to use them effectively and report findings accurately.
    • Defect Identification & Reporting: Learn what defects look like in the field, how to evaluate their severity, and how to communicate them clearly without alarming clients.
    • Building Field Confidence: Master client interaction, agent communication, and time management so you can conduct your own inspections smoothly from day one.

    • Preparing for OAHI Certification? New inspectors aiming to join the Ontario Association of Home Inspectors (OAHI) must complete practical field walkthroughs and peer-reviewed report verifications. Our 1-on-1 mentorship helps you build real-world experience, practice standard report writing, and gain the field confidence needed to work toward your professional designations.

    A home inspection is not a warranty or guarantee, because a home inspection is a visual inspection only. It is not a forensic inspection that goes deep into issues, removes walls, measures data, or takes apart systems. However, it can help alleviate some fears during an already stressful time.

  • Our Focus: Comfort, Safety, and Performance

    We focus on finding issues related to the energy efficiency of a home, potential safety risks, and whether a system functions as intended. A home inspection covers both the interior and exterior of a house, involving numerous systems. A home inspector must possess a wide range of knowledge, evaluating each system for comfort, safety, and performance.
