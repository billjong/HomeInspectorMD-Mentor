    Why Choose 1-on-1 Field Coaching?

    Passing your exams is a great first step, but gaining real-world field experience is where most new inspectors struggle. While corporate training companies and college instructors offer group field trips, they often leave you standing in the back of a crowd watching someone else work.
    Here is how personal 1-on-1 mentorship compares to traditional training options:

    Detailed Breakdown of Your Training Options

    Option 1: Dedicated 1-on-1 Field Coaching (My Service)

    My mentorship is built around a simple principle: you learn best by doing, not by watching from the back of a crowd.

    • 100% Dedicated Attention: It’s just you and me. Every question you have gets answered immediately, and you are guided through every step of the inspection process directly.
    • Hands-On Advanced Tool Practice: You won't just see a thermal camera or a drone—you’ll put your hands on the equipment, learn how to operate it, and understand how to interpret the results during real inspections.

    • Flexible & Accessible: We work together to find inspection dates and property walkthroughs that fit your timeline, without waiting months for a class to open up.
    • Maximum Value for Your Money: Instead of paying top dollar to share an instructor with nine other people, every dollar goes toward direct, focused field coaching tailored specifically to your needs.
    • Your House, No Time Limit: You provide the property—whether it’s your own home, a friend’s, or a relative’s house. In exchange, there is no rigid time limit. We take all the time needed to thoroughly inspect the property, practice with tools, analyze defects, and answer all your questions without rushing.

    Some students seek out college instructors for extra field training after their course finishes. While usually cheaper than corporate packages, these sessions face real logistical challenges:

    • Difficulty Finding Houses: College instructors often struggle to secure suitable homes for field walkthroughs, leading to long delays between sessions.
    • Group Distractions: Even with smaller groups of 3 to 5 students, the instructor’s time is divided. You rarely get to perform a full inspection from start to finish yourself.
    • Older Inspection Methods: Many traditional instructors stick strictly to basic curriculum standards, missing out on teaching modern equipment like thermal imaging or drone technology.
    • Rushed Walkthroughs: Group sessions are bound by rigid, multi-student time slots, meaning you rarely get to take your time or perform a full inspection yourself.

    Option 3: Major Corporate Training Companies

    Corporate training programs are well-known, but their field training model has significant drawbacks for practical learners:

    • Crowded Groups: With up to 10 students crammed into a single home, you spend most of your time standing in line or looking over shoulders while the instructor points out defects.
    • High Cost, Low Hands-On Time: Charging around $1,000 for a rushed 2-day session (covering about three houses), the cost per hour of actual tool handling is extremely high.
    • Outdated Technology Scope: They stick to traditional visual inspections and generally do not offer thermal imaging (IR) camera practice or drone training—tools that modern clients and agents expect.
    • Rigid Schedules: Sessions are offered infrequently, forcing you to wait months or adjust your schedule to fit theirs.
    • Strict Time Limits: Inspections are strictly capped (often around 2 hours per home), rushing you through the house without time to ask detailed questions or thoroughly practice.

  • The 1-on-1 Field Coaching Advantage


    1. Dedicated 1-on-1 Attention (No Crowds)
    You get 100% of my time, direct guidance on every system, and immediate answers without competing for attention.

    2. Hands-On Advanced Tech Training (IR & Drones)
    Get hands-on experience operating essential modern tools—including Infrared (Thermal) Cameras and Aerial Drones—so you can deliver the high-value inspection reports that modern clients and agents expect.

    3. Meticulous Attention to Detail
    A great inspection isn't about rushing—it's about knowing where defects hide. I teach a disciplined, systematic routine that helps you catch the subtle details others miss, building thorough habits that protect both you and your clients.

    4. Complete & Client-Friendly Report Writing
    Finding defects is only half the job; reporting them clearly makes or breaks your business. Learn how to write thorough, professional reports that explain technical issues simply—without causing panic or alienating real estate agents.

    5. No Rigid Time Limits (Learn Without Rushing)
    By providing the property (your own home, or a friend’s or relative’s house), we eliminate rigid time caps. We take all the time you need to inspect the home, practice with tools, and refine your process without feeling rushed.
