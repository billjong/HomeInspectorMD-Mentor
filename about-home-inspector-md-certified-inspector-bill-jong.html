  • Home Inspection Education

    Sheridan: Home Inspection Certificate Program

    The Sheridan program is partnered with Carson Dunlop to provide all the training materials for the course. Carson Dunlop has been in the home inspection profession since 1978. The Home Inspection Certificate Program is a 10-course program covering all the systems in a residential home. Graduated with a grade point average of A+/4.0 (transcript).

           

    InterNACHI: Certified Professional Inspector

    Founded in 1994, InterNACHI® is The International Association of Certified Home Inspectors and “the world’s largest trade organization of residential and commercial property inspectors.” InterNACHI® School is a Member College of the National Association of Career Colleges (NACC) of Canada.

    Preparing for OAHI Certification?

    New inspectors aiming to join the Ontario Association of Home Inspectors (OAHI) must complete practical field walkthroughs and peer-reviewed report verifications. Our 1-on-1 mentorship helps you build real-world experience, practice standard report writing, and gain the field confidence needed to work toward your professional designations.
